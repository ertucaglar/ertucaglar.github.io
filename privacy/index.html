Privacy Policy

The Adam Game · Last updated: 5 November 2026

The Adam Game is a mobile game made by Ertuğrul Çağlar, an individual developer based in Turkey, who is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: ertucaglar@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name or email.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • Ads and analytics are provided by Google and Unity, who process device identifiers and usage data.
  • Video ads are optional: you only see one when you choose to watch it for a reward.
  • In Europe, ads use personal data and analytics run only if you agree.

We run no servers of our own and receive no data that identifies you. The daily reminder notification is scheduled on your device and sends no data. You can turn it off in your device settings.

Sharing diagnostics (only if you choose to)

The game keeps a short record of your last 50 battles on your device (at most 10 MB): the level, battle times, which units were sent and lost, food and soul levels, the Gold earned by source, the result and the game version. It contains no name, email, device or advertising identifier, location, purchase receipt or copy of your save file, and it is never sent automatically. Only if you tap Settings → Share Diagnostics does the game prepare these files and open your device's share sheet; you choose where they go (for example an email to us), or cancel. Settings → Delete Diagnostics erases the record. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), KVKK Art. 5/1), given by sharing.

Why this data is processed

  • Your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), KVKK Art. 5/1), where it is required: personalised ads and analytics.
  • Our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5/2-f): showing non-personalised ads to fund a free game, keeping the game stable, preventing ad fraud.
  • Performing a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5/2-c): delivering what you buy in the game.

Your choices

  • Europe (EEA, UK, Switzerland): we ask for your consent before personal data is used for ads or analytics, using Google's consent tool. If you do not agree, analytics stay off. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy settings in the game; this does not affect anything done before.
  • United States: where state law gives you the right to opt out, the same Privacy settings option lets you do so.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

The game works the same whatever you choose, and declining earns or costs you nothing in the game.

Keeping and deleting data

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game; deleting the game erases it. Google and Unity keep the data they process for the periods set out in their own policies, linked above; Unity keeps analytics data for 13 months by default. Deleting the game stops all further collection. To ask for data linked to your device to be deleted, write to us.

Security

Data sent by the game to the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We keep no copy of it ourselves.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and Article 11 of Turkey's KVKK) you may have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to access, correct, delete or move it, to object to its use, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Board). We hold no account linked to you, so to act on a request we may need you to send the identifier shown by your device. Write to ertucaglar@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

International transfers

Google and Unity may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards in their own terms, such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

Tercihinizi oyunda Ayarlar → Gizlilik ayarları bölümünden istediğiniz zaman değiştirebilir ya da geri alabilirsiniz; bu, daha önce yapılanları etkilemez.

Oyun, seçiminiz ne olursa olsun aynı çalışır; reddetmek oyunda size bir şey kazandırmaz ya da kaybettirmez.
